Chores that come back on time

Cadence is a chore tracker for everything at home that comes around again: the laundry, the plants, the filters, the bins.

Most reminder apps repeat on a fixed date whether or not you did the thing. Cadence counts from when you last did a task. Water the plants on Tuesday and a three-day task is next due on Friday.

What it does

  • Today at a glance. What's due now, and what's coming up.
  • Schedules that fit real life. Repeat a task after you last did it, or keep it on fixed weekdays.
  • Quiet reminders. Only when something is due, with quiet hours, snooze and Away.
  • A shared household. Invite family or roommates, assign tasks, and see who did what.
  • Yours to keep. Works without an account. Sign in to back up your tasks for free.

Free and Cadence Plus

The free plan has 10 recurring tasks on your own list and 10 in a household, unlimited one-off tasks, and a household of 2.

Cadence Plus removes the limit on recurring tasks for you and your household, allows up to 6 people, and adds automatic turns, a weekly look at how the work is shared, and short plans for when you have 5, 10, 20 or 30 minutes. One subscription covers the payer's own list and one household. The app shows your price before you buy.
